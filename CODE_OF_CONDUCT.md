# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

## In short

- Be respectful and patient. People here range from first-time builders to
  professional engineers, and many write in a second language.
- Critique the work, not the person. Safety concerns are always welcome and are
  never an attack.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, or
  publishing anyone's private information, including their location, network
  details or photos of their property.
- Help keep everyone's builds safe: do not post other people's secrets, keys or
  addresses, even if they leaked them by accident; point them to
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other space where you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer (GitHub user
`ysemenikhin`), using the private vulnerability report form described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if you need a private channel. Reports are handled
confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code, and may block contributors who
repeatedly or seriously break it, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant 2.1.
